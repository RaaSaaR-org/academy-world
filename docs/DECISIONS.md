# Founder decisions

These answers settle the open questions in GDD §13. Where the GDD says otherwise, this file wins.

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|----------|----------|
| D1 | Du or Sie? | **Du everywhere**, in both modes. Entscheider mode changes content depth, never the form of address. Drop every "Sie" variant the GDD drafts; a `ModeString` exists only where the *content* differs between modes. |
| D2 | Third-party robots (Unitree G1/H1)? | **Superseded by Phase 7: see D15.** **None in v1.** No Unitree models, names or look-alikes. "Hersteller X" and every other non-EMI robot is procedural and unbranded. |
| D3 | Which EMI? | **EMI Rover, 1.55 m** concept size (17+2 joints, from EmAI's EMI design parameters, params.py / PRD), as already built by the engine's EMI kit. |
| D4 | Primary use context | **Public web experience.** Self-paced, opened from a link. No kiosk or trainer mode in v1. |
| D5 | Ending / certificate link | GDD proposal stands: one neutral line plus an academy.emai.dev link, no prices, no contact data. |
| D6 | Robot-vendor names in text *(Claude's default, awaiting founder confirmation)* | **Superseded by Phase 7: see D15, D32 and D34.** No real robot-vendor brand names in player-facing text, signs or plaques. Historical facts stay only in anonymised, sourced form ("ein japanischer Roboterhersteller …"). Statistics bodies (IFR, Eurostat, Destatis, IW Köln, BAuA, DGUV) may be cited. |
| D7 | Four trim rotors under the Kanzeln *(Claude's default, awaiting founder confirmation)* | Keep them. They were added by the art pass and are not in the GDD. The island is still a hexacopter with six main lift rotors, M1–M6. |
| D8 | Stars in a learning game *(Claude's default from the Phase 4 fun pass, awaiting founder confirmation)* | Stars allowed: 1–3 per station, rating the player's own play and method, never a robot or a vendor. Personal bests stay in this browser. No leaderboards. (FUN-PLAN §10) |
| D9 | Recamán sculpture and arrival look *(Claude's defaults from ART-V2 §9, awaiting founder confirmation; amended in Phase 5)* | Q1: unlit arcs are warm brushed aluminium, lit arcs brand orange (not orange at 0.2 opacity). Q2: no golden-angle swing of the mark at the finale; a one-time laser sweep along the lit lines instead. Q3: sun azimuth stays 295° (not 255°). Q4: mark radius R = 10.5 m. Q5 *(Phase 5)*: low PV tracker rows along the Allee and the north spoke replace the solar trees (the founder: "the trees make no sense"); they carry no label, so the game makes no claim that they power the island or its flight. *(Phase 5)*: the anamorphic fan of Q1–Q4's sculpture is replaced by a planar relief, see D10. |
| D10 | Logo sculpture *(coordinator's default, Phase 5, awaiting founder confirmation)* | The Recamán mark on the tower is a **shallow relief readable from every front view**: ring and 17 arcs as 0.42 m tubes in one plane, 0.45 m in front of a machined dial, held by a collar, a two-strut yoke on pivots at 3/9 o'clock and a hub. The founder can overrule; the rc2 fan is in git at `721765c`. Note for the brand review: the white pivot housings at 3 and 9 o'clock are visible from the front just outside the disc edge. They are physical mounts like the lattice behind, but strictly they fall inside the logo manual's 25 % clear space. The dial's front edge stays #141414 (no light outline). |
| D11 | Kielraum look *(Claude's default, Phase 5, awaiting founder confirmation)* | The Kielraum is a dark control room: smoked glass and a graphite frame instead of tinted glass and an alu truss, a graphite well under the gimbal, interior light 0.45 (ART-V2 §4.6 asked for 0.6) and a catwalk grating #77726D (ART-V2: #A8A29E), so the white gimbal and the stations' screens lead. |
| D12 | Should visitors whose browser is not set to German start in English? *(Claude's default, Phase 6, plan F1, awaiting founder confirmation)* | **Yes, on the first visit only.** With no saved language, the game starts in English when the browser's language does not start with "de"; German stays the house default (also when the browser reports no language). A saved choice and `?lang=` win. The language switch stays on the title and in the pause menu, now labelled „Deutsch · English“ on a dark backplate. |
| D13 | Should returning visitors skip the 25 s ascent and resume near where they stopped? *(Claude's default, Phase 6, plan F2, awaiting founder confirmation)* | **Superseded by Phase 7: see D26 (the ascent is retired).** **Yes.** After one complete ascent (watched to the end, not skipped and not under reduced motion; island progress flag `ascentSeen`), Start skips it; the title offers „Anflug ansehen“ / „Watch the approach“ to see it again (hidden under reduced motion). The player starts at the gate of the zone entered last, or at the Landedeck if there is none, with the toast „Weiter im Regelgarten“ / „Continue in the Control Garden“ („in der“ for Greifwerkstatt and Dunkelfabrik). The title says „Weiterspielen“ / „Continue“ whenever any progress exists (stamps, stars, capsules or question cards). `?skipIntro`, `?zone`, `?pos`, `?world=0` and a dev reload keep the first-visit flow. A save from rc3 has no `ascentSeen`, so that visitor sees the ascent once more. |
| D14 | Should the settings get what GDD §5.10 promises: separate volumes and a subtitle size? *(Claude's default, Phase 6, plan F3, awaiting founder confirmation)* | **Yes.** A new pause tab „Ton & Text“ / „Sound & text“ holds the master volume plus three sliders: „Atmosphäre & Musik“ (ambience and zone beds), „Effekte“ (UI, foley, cues) and „EMI“ (voice). They trim each bus after its own gain, so the ducking still works. „Textgröße“ 100 / 125 / 150 % scales the subtitles and the in-play HUD (objective, prompts, hints, toasts, result banner, panels, world labels, captions); the Pass, info cards, the pause menu and the compass keep their size. Older saves keep their values, and the new settings start at 100 %. |
| D15 | Real robots in the game (lifts D2 and D6)? *(confirmed by the founder 2026-10-17)* | **Yes.** Real robots appear by name with their **real 3D models**: official or open models whose licence allows a free public web game published by a company, always with attribution (credits page, plaque line, `licenses/`). Never a hand-modelled look-alike. Plan: `docs/PHASE7-PLAN.md`. |
| D16 | How does Phase 7 add the new content? *(confirmed by the founder 2026-10-17)* | **Upgrade + add.** Fitting stations get the real robots (Lern-Dojo → SO-101 with imitation and language commands, Regelgarten → Open Duck Mini RL); new: a Unitree world-model hall („Traumhangar“) and a showroom („Schauhalle“); weak stations are reworked or cut. The Entdecker route may grow to roughly 80–100 min. |
| D17 | Prices in the showroom? *(confirmed by the founder 2026-10-17)* | **Price ranges (bands) with source and date**, not exact list prices and not "no prices". D5's no-prices rule is about EmAI's own services. Bands: „unter 1.000“ · „1.000–10.000“ · „10.000–50.000“ · „50.000–150.000“ · „über 150.000“ · „auf Anfrage“ (Listenpreis, US-$ oder €, ohne Steuern und Versand). |

| D23 | What gates the lift-off, and how is progress shown? *(decided by the lead on the founder's instruction „do what you think looks the best“, 2026-10-17; the founder's answer to the gate question: „i mean the whole world“. Replaces the second-pass default „six island ◆ in both modes“)* | **The whole world, through three systems the island needs to fly** (plan §3.1). **AUFTRIEB** (lift): the six main rotors M1–M6, each started by the ◆ station of its island zone. **ENERGIE** (energy): the **Startakku**, charged by the Sonnenacker ◆ „Erntetag“ from the agrivoltaic field, so the farm harvests tomatoes *and* power. **NAVIGATION**: the **Flugmodell**, the world model the island uses to fly blind through the cloud bank, installed by the Traumhangar ◆ „Traumdaten“ (the training mix with the best closed-loop result, never the offline winner). **Entdecker:** the lift-off needs the 6 island ◆ + the Sonnenacker ◆ + the Traumhangar ◆ + a signed F3. Both ground ◆ were already on the route, so it stays ≈ 94 min. **Entscheider:** the 6 island ◆ + F3 + the Schaufenster stop (≈ 29 min, D41). Right after the Probestart the Bodencrew supplies energy and navigation, labelled „von der Bodencrew bereitgestellt“, and the Schaufenster shows the recorded Sonnenacker and Traumhangar runs behind them. A short Sonnenacker ◆ on that route was considered and rejected: the round trip plus the station costs ≥ 4 min, past 30. Playing the optional Sonnenacker or Traumhangar detour replaces the crew label with the player's own. The supply follows the current mode (the pause-menu switch). **Visible from minute one:** the failed **Probestart** turns all three red on the Libelle plinth (six rotor lamps, a 10-segment Startakku bar, a nav lamp), in view at the lever. The six crown beacons of the tower repeat the rotors, and the needle light repeats the nav lamp (nothing on the collar, which sits in the mark's clear space, D10). They light up amber → teal as the player progresses. The Revier opens at 1/6 rotors and the Startprüfung (KIEL) at 4/6, both unchanged. In the lift-off the Startakku bar is full at the spin-up, and the nav lamp switches to active before the whiteout. Inside the cloud the Flugmodell's teal ghost horizon, 2 s ahead (TRAUM), visibly guides the climb. **Never blocking:** everything else enriches the lift-off and never gates it: the other stations, the Schauhalle, Kreislaufhof, Unkrautacker, side stations, eggs and trims. Online trims add notes and plumes, stamped sites send their people to the apron, „geprüft“ systems keep a halo and earned robots ride along. The H2 stays optional (D21). If a ◆ station (island or ground) cannot be loaded after two retries, the Bodencrew brings its system online („Bodencrew · ohne Kennlinie“, no stamp, no stars), so a failed download never blocks the goal. **Honesty:** the Startakku is FIKTION like the island. The field's kWh stay RICHTWERT, and the game never converts them into thrust or flight time. |

**Engineering choices of the Phase 6 fix round (recorded so they can be revisited):**
- MSAA stays 2× at a display pixel ratio of 1.5 (the plan said 0×): without it, far railings broke into dashes (screenshot A/B of the QA-04 view). 4× → 2× still saves about half of the MSAA cost.
- The low preset now renders through the same grade as medium and high (no MSAA, no bloom), so a quality switch no longer relinks about 50 shader programs. Compared with rc3, low gains a light vignette and its beacons look like medium's.
- The lit curved glass (glass parapets, Libelle dish, Kielraum shell, tower glass) stays two-pass: single-pass rendering visibly changed the Ausguck balustrade and the Kielraum shell.
- The Messwand in the Kielraum keeps its mipmaps (the plan suggested dropping them): without them, thin strokes and small text broke up from the lift and the far catwalk. The 4 MB-per-frame upload target is met anyway.
- *(Awaiting founder confirmation; a game-design change.)* Sensordom A (Messbrille): pressing E on an object the active sensor view cannot see is now ignored, so a miss can no longer happen. ★★ and ★★★ therefore count view switches instead of misses: ★★ at most 6 switches, ★★★ at most 6 switches in ≤ 90 s (before: at most 2 misses).

**Decided by the lead in the Phase 7 plan (engineering calls, recorded so they can be revisited; details in `docs/PHASE7-PLAN.md`; second pass after the critiques C1 and C2):**
- Areas beyond the six lobes (the Schauhalle, the Sonnenacker, the Traumhangar, the use-case scenes) reuse the zone contract with new plot fields (`kind`, `frame`, `shape`, `tiers`, `interior`). `ctx.zones.list()` returns the six lobes by default, so the rc4 island code that builds masts, railings and pads from it stays unchanged.
- Every area is split into a small eager `manifest.ts` (id, kind, frame, stations with `star`, `rev`, `mastery` and their route role) and lazy code. This keeps the first load ≤ 1.75 MB and lets rotor states, the star pool and the save migration work before any zone code has loaded.
- One engine call sets the altitude and fans out to the ground, the slab, the clouds, the fog, the colliders and the suspension.
