`SIGRobotics-UIUC/LeKiwi` @ efa608d: `LICENSE.txt` is Apache-2.0 for the whole repository; `CITATION.cff` repeats it and names the authors; the README, BOM and the URDF / mesh folders carry no other terms. **Green**: `lekiwi-base` ships (base only; the arm on top is `so101`, mounted at link `Base_08q-v1`, manifest `mounts.arm`). ### Decisions applied to the models

- D32: SO-101 printed parts white #F5F5F4, servos black #1A1A1A, the moving jaw (follower) and the trigger (leader) orange #FF6700 (the source prints them yellow).

- R3: link inertials (mass, centre of mass in the link frame) from the source in the manifest (`inertials`) and the GLB extras, wherever the source has them.
